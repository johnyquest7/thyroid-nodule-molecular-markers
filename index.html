Clinical Molecular Diagnostic Panels Reference

Methodological differences, gene coverage, and reporting frameworks across clinical testing platforms.

ThyroSeq v3 UPMC / CBLPath

Targeted DNA + RNA Next-Generation Sequencing

  • 112 Genes: Evaluates SNVs, indels, gene fusions, expression, and copy number alterations (CNAs).
  • CNA Algorithmic Scoring: Specifically calibrated to identify genome-wide near-haploidization in Oncocytic (Hürthle cell) carcinoma.
  • Risk Stratification: High Risk (BRAF, TERT, RET M918T, TP53, ALK) vs Currently Available / RAS-like vs Low Risk.
  • Performance: ~94% Sensitivity, ~82% Specificity in Bethesda III/IV nodules.
Afirma GSC & XA Veracyte

Whole-Transcriptome RNA-Seq & Machine Learning

  • GSC (Genomic Sequencing Classifier): Machine-learning RNA classifier for primary Benign vs. Suspicious rule-out calls.
  • Xpression Atlas (XA): Detects 905 variants and 235 fusions across 593 genes on GSC-suspicious or Bethesda V/VI aspirates.
  • MTC & Parathyroid Classifiers: Dedicated RNA modules for medullary carcinoma and parathyroid tissue detection.
  • Performance: ~91% Sensitivity, ~68% Specificity (NPV ~96%).
ThyGeNEXT / ThyraMIR Interpace Biosciences

Dual Mutational Panel + MicroRNA Risk Classifier

  • ThyGeNEXT: Targeted NGS panel identifying mutations and gene fusions in BRAF, RAS, RET, PAX8::PPARG, etc.
  • ThyraMIR v2: Pairwise microRNA expression profiling to classify indeterminate nodules as Positive, Moderate, or Negative risk.
  • Dual Rule-in / Rule-out: High NPV for rule-out when microRNA is negative, high PPV when strong oncogenic drivers detected.
